About me

I graduated from the Malawi University of Science and Technology in 2025 with a BSc in Geo-Information Systems and Earth Observation Science, and I hold a Malawi CAA remote pilot licence.

I work as a data analyst as well as a GIS specialist. Some of my data comes from drones, satellites and GNSS surveys, and some from databases, permit records and spreadsheets. Either way, I clean it, analyse it and present it as maps, models and dashboards that decision-makers can use.

I am looking for a role where data supports real decisions, in sectors such as agriculture, water, environment, disaster risk and urban planning.
